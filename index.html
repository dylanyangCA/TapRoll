NFC attendance for teachers

Take roll
in one tap.

TapRoll is an attendance app for teachers. Each student gets an NFC tag. When they tap it on your phone or a reader at the door, TapRoll marks them present or tardy for the class that’s in session.

Free for teachers. TapRoll runs in your web browser, so there’s nothing to install.

How it works

Set it up once for the school year. After that, students check themselves in.

  1. Add your bell schedule

    Enter each period with its bell times, then paste in your class roster. TapRoll uses the bell times to tell which class a tap belongs to and who’s tardy.

  2. Give each student a tag

    Write a tag in a few seconds with an Android phone, or link a tag the student already has. Tags hold a random code, not a name.

  3. Students tap in

    TapRoll marks each student present or tardy, with live counts for every period. Change any mark by hand, and download attendance as a spreadsheet.

Works with what you have

Use whichever fits your classroom door. You can mix them.

  • Android phone

    Chrome on Android reads and writes NFC tags.

  • iPhone

    Tap a student’s tag on your iPhone, open the link, and they’re checked in.

  • USB reader

    Plug a keyboard-style 13.56 MHz reader into any computer.

  • By hand

    Mark present, tardy, absent or excused when a tag stays home.

Tags: NTAG213, NTAG215 or NTAG216 stickers, cards and key fobs.

For the whole school

School admins and registrars sign in to a School office that shows every class at once.

  • Live attendance

    Watch every class fill in as students tap in, and see who’s absent today across the school.

  • Rosters from Excel

    Import a spreadsheet to put students in each teacher’s classes, or add students by name.

  • Teachers linked

    Invite teachers by email. They join from their own account and can leave anytime.

  • One tag per student

    A tag written in any class checks the student in to all of their classes.

See it with made-up teachers: open the example classroom, then choose School office.

Built for student privacy

Attendance records are student data. TapRoll collects only what it needs to take attendance.

Read the Privacy Policy
  • Tags hold only a random code. No names, and they can’t track location.
  • Classes are private. Each teacher’s classes open only from their own account, and from their school office if they choose to join their school.
  • No ads. Student information is never sold.
  • Google sign-in is only for signing in. TapRoll gets your name and email address and can’t see anything else in your Google account.
